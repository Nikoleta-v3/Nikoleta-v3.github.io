<h1>Franziska Lesigang</h1>

Franziska is a PhD student at IT:U in Linz, Austria, and is co-supervised by me.
She is investigating repeated games with multiple players.

Franziska visited the RIKEN Center for Computational Science (R-CCS) in Kobe,
between March and June 2025, as part of the [international internship program](https://www.r-ccs.riken.jp/en/about/careers/internship/).

Check out more on her personal website:
[https://franzilesi.github.io](https://franzilesi.github.io).

<h2>Publications</h2>

- Can I Afford to Remember Less Than You? Best Responses in Repeated Additive Games. 2025. *Economics Letters*.

<h2>Master's</h2>

Franziska joined the *Group Dynamics of Social Behaviour* from August to
September 2024 for an internship and was co-supervised by me and
Dr. Christian Hilbe.

Following the internship, Franziska worked with us on her master's thesis,
titled *"Nash Equilibria in Finite Action Games with Conditional Strategies"*.
