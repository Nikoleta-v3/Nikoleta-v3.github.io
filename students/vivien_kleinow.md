<h1> Vivien Kleinow </h1>

Vivien worked with me initially as a research assistant and then for her master's thesis, titled  
*“Mapping Computational Neuroscience: Exploring Topics and Evolution through Scientometrics,”*  
for Computational Neuroscience, Department of Neuroscience, University of Oldenburg.

The objective of the thesis project was to utilize a scientometric approach to
comprehensively understand the current state of the field of computational neuroscience.
This involved evaluating the multidisciplinary nature of the field, identifying prominent
research topics, and exploring its evolution over time.

Vivien is currently a PhD student at Hamburg University of Technology, Germany.  
Check out her profile here: [TUHH Profile – Vivien Kleinow](https://www.tuhh.de/odce/staff/vivien-kleinow).
